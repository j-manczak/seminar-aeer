6. CORE RESEARCH QUESTION
The main research question is:
Did the shutdown of a nuclear power plant cause a measurable change in downstream river water temperature and dissolved oxygen compared with an upstream station?
The basic identification strategy is:
              BEFORE        AFTER

UPSTREAM         A             B
DOWNSTREAM       C             D
The Difference-in-Differences estimator is:
DiD = (D - C) - (B - A)
In words:
Change downstream after shutdown minus change upstream after shutdown.
The shutdown is the treatment.
The upstream/downstream distinction provides the spatial comparison.
Do NOT incorrectly describe downstream itself as the treatment.
